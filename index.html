Established in 2012 by executive advisor Mr. Kaelo Motshotlwane, Mills Accountants is a premier financial consultancy practices and an elite division of Gopolang Katlego 09 Trading CC. We orchestrate fiscal growth throughout Johannesburg and partner with corporate clients across South Africa's primary economic provinces.
